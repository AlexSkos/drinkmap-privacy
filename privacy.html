Valencia Fountain Map is developed and operated by Aleksandr Skosyrskii. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, processed, and shared when you use the Valencia Fountain Map mobile application (the "App").

Valencia Fountain Map helps users find public drinking fountains in Valencia, Spain, view fountain locations on a map, and report location information or issues.

1. Accounts

Valencia Fountain Map does not require you to create an account, register, or provide your name in order to use the App.

2. Location Data

The App may request access to your device's foreground location in order to show your position on the map and help you find nearby drinking fountains.

Location permission is optional. You may deny or revoke location permission at any time through your device settings.

Valencia Fountain Map does not use background location tracking. Your device location is used to provide the map and nearby-fountain functionality and is not stored by the developer as a personal user profile.

Google and its advertising partners may process certain device and usage information in connection with advertising. This may include information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location derived from network information, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, depending on your device settings, region, and consent choices.

In regions where consent is required, including the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, and Switzerland, the App uses Google's consent management system to request and manage advertising consent.

Depending on your consent choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited as required by applicable law and Google advertising policies.

Where available, you can review or change your privacy and advertising consent choices by using the Privacy option inside the App.

This information is used only to respond to your request, investigate reported issues, maintain the App, or improve the accuracy of fountain information.
